# V0.8 Pass 083: Aquarium Habitat and Vegetation Overhaul

Base: the approved Pass 082/082B reef benchmark. Scope: the personal aquarium only (the outer
waters and other worlds are untouched). Rollback: `?reef=original` (the accepted Pass 080 tank);
`?reef=benchmark` shows the Pass 082 section alone.

## 1. Audit of the accepted aquarium (Pass 080)

Matched views: `docs/screenshots/v08-pass083/` (left: Pass 080, right: Pass 083). The audit of the
accepted tank, from the same fixed views and the office's glass:

1. **A field of separate, rounded boulders.** 34 rocks of one family (six procedural templates,
   scaled and rotated) stand apart on the sand. From any swimming view they read as one repeated
   object: rounded, olive, the same scale, with the same "muffin" undercut. There is no ledge, no
   overhang, no step from one mass to the next, and no structure taller than its width.
2. **Isolated objects on a large sand floor.** The rocks cluster loosely on both banks, but the gaps
   between them are sand, so each bank is a row of separate stones, not a reef.
3. **Corals perched, not attached.** The hardscape colonies sit singly on the crowns of boulders,
   like ornaments placed on top; nothing grows out of a face, a ledge or a crevice, and there is no
   hierarchy of large colonies, secondary structures and small attached growth.
4. **Needle meadows.** The seagrass beds are single straight blades instanced one by one, plus a
   shorter layer; they read as vertical needles standing in a disc.
5. **Plants as specks.** The algae and seagrass sprigs stand one by one on open sand, too small and
   too evenly spread to read as growth.
6. **Uniform scatter.** 55 pebbles and some 600 shells and rubble fragments are spread over the
   whole floor, so the open sand reads as littered rather than calm.
7. **Monotone heights.** Almost every rock crown sits at 1.5-3.5 m; nothing leads the eye.
8. **Kept as they are** (not in the brief, or already approved): the Pass 082 benchmark section,
   the ornaments and decorations, the crevice outcrop and the passage, the glass backdrop reliefs.

## 2. Method

The approved Pass 082 method, applied to every other reef rock (tools/art/pass083):

- **Grouping.** The 28 remaining rocks are grouped into seven formations by where they already
  stand (hull gaps under 1.5 m): two massifs at the back of each bank (the West massif with the
  moray's den, the East massif around the crevice), two terraces along the banks, a channel terrace
  (the frogfish's rock) and two satellite outcrops at the front.
- **Composed masses, inside the accepted hulls.** Each rock becomes a low undercut plinth, a strongly
  tilted bedding slab offset to one side (its overhanging edge is the ledge), and a smaller offset
  cap or a pair of pinnacles; some carry a cantilevered shelf. Every mass is clipped to its rock's
  accepted hull (grown 0.12 m), so fish, the swimmer, creature homes and the tank editor keep the
  space they already know. Rock heights are varied on purpose (62-100 % of each hull), so neighbours
  step instead of repeating: massifs tall, terraces stepping down to the channel, outcrops low.
- **Saddles.** Low bridges of cemented rubble (at most 0.7 m high) cross the narrow sand gaps
  between rocks of one formation, so a formation reads as one structure. They are the only new
  solid volume, and are covered by the swimming, fish and placement tests.
- **Detail and painting** are Pass 082B's: fracture planes, flattened fossil lobes, porosity and
  pockets, grottos facing open water, and form-driven colour (fresh fractures, worn edges, silted
  hollows, turf, coralline crusts, stains, a dusted foot).
- **Creature homes sculpted in.** The moray's den is a tunnel through solid rock, open at its mouth;
  the frogfish's perch is a flat-topped seat at the height it already rests at.

## 3. Composition: a hierarchy, not a scatter

| Formation            | Accepted rocks        | Role                                                                                          |
| -------------------- | --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| West massif          | 3, 9, 21, 25, 33      | Back-left major structure: tall, layered, the moray's den, a brain coral crowning it          |
| East massif          | 2, 8, 18, 28          | Back-right major structure around the crevice; tabular Acropora on its crest                  |
| West terraces        | 1, 5, 15, 27          | Stepped bank leading from the West massif toward the channel, with a grotto and a crest table |
| East terraces        | 6, 10, 12, 14, 16, 20 | Long stepped bank; the staghorn thicket where the accepted colony stood                       |
| East channel terrace | 4, 22, 26, 32, 34     | Low terrace by the channel: the frogfish's seat and a tabular colony                          |

| East outcrops        | 24, 30                | Satellite patch reef by the log and statue                                                    |

Within each formation the life follows the same hierarchy as Pass 082:

1. **Hero colonies** stand where the accepted hardscape corals stood (their colliders keep their
   meaning): a brain coral (West massif), two tabular Acropora (East massif, channel terrace) and
   a staghorn thicket (East terraces), plus crest tables on the West massif and terraces.
2. **Secondary structures**: encrusting massive fields across the ledges (a few large fields,
   more medium and small ones around them) and plating colonies tiered out of the channel-facing
   walls.
3. **Attached growth**: corymbose bushes, leather and finger soft corals, tube sponges on the
   shoulders and encrusting sponges on the shaded faces, densest on the massifs, sparse on the
   outcrops.

No new species: every colony is a Pass 082B family. Heights, sizes and orientations vary per site
(each colony grows along the face it stands on).

**Negative space.** The central channel (|x| < 2.6 m), the spawn, the open sand in front of the
statue, chest and wreck, and every creature-home approach stay open; the satellites are separated
from the banks by sand so the front of the tank reads as open water.

## 4. Vegetation

- **Meadows.** The needle meadows (single straight blades plus a short layer) are replaced, in the
  same eight beds, by turtle-grass **tufts**: 8-14 strap blades rising from one node, splaying,
  arching over and twisting, with yellowing and epiphyte-filmed old blades. Beds are drifts of
  tufts: dense and tall in the middle, short and ragged at the edge, with a gap or two.
- **Rock-foot clusters.** Grass, Halimeda, Caulerpa and red algae gather where sand meets each
  formation's foot (44 clusters), and Halimeda and red algae root in upward pockets and crevices of
  the rock.
- **Accepted sprigs** (turtle grass, Caulerpa, Halimeda, red macroalgae from the plant system) keep
  their placements, species and fish interests but are redrawn with the organic templates. Those
  that stood on an accepted rock crown are reseated on the composed rock below them (or withheld
  when no rock is beneath them). Sea fans and soft corals keep their templates (the seahorse grips
  its sea fan).
- **Templates** (unit height, per-blade phase, the shared current-driven sway): Grass, GrassShort,
  Caulerpa (a runner with curving leaf-like blades), Halimeda (Pass 082) and a fleshy, forking,
  curling red alga.

## 5. Loose scatter

The 55 accepted pebbles and the shells and rubble are kept where they lie near reef (within 1.3 m
of rock, the crevice or an ornament) and withheld out on open sand, so small debris gathers where
reef meets sand and the open floor reads as calm sand.

**Why it is better than Pass 080.** The banks now read as two reefs, each a hierarchy of tall
massifs, stepped terraces and low outcrops, instead of a field of 28 same-sized olive boulders.
Rock has ledges, overhangs, bedding and fractures and is pale, mineral limestone; corals grow out of
its faces and over its ledges rather than sitting on its crowns; the meadows are arching tufts
instead of needles; and the sand between and in front of the reefs is clean, so the open water and
the channel read as deliberate space. Through the office glass the tank reads as a planted reef
aquarium rather than a gravel bed of stones.

## 8. Compatibility

- **Collision.** Each composed mass and saddle has its own convex part (the hull of its rendered
  vertices); they replace the accepted rock hulls for the swimmer and the fish together (one shared
  list), and the accepted hulls return with `?reef=original` or a load failure. Rock above the sand
  is covered by solid parts (> 97 % of visible vertices), and no part reaches the channel.
- **Fish and creatures.** Interest points covered by new rock are withheld; every creature-home
  approach, the arch passage and the full wreck circuit stay clear (`integrationRoutes`); simulated
  fish stay out of the rock over a 20 s run; the moray's den is open with rock all round its body;
  the frogfish rests on its seat within 0.2 m of its perch; the anemone, seahorse and nudibranch
  homes are unchanged.
- **Customization.** The placement context (keep-out zones, routes, the channel) is unchanged.
  Decorations saved on open sand stay valid on the habitat's visible surface (> 95 % of a grid of
  placements valid on the accepted tank; the rest lay against a rock and are kept in the save, not
  erased, by the existing kept-entries rule). The save format is unchanged.
- **Personal tank versus the ocean.** The habitat stays an aquarium: the glass, equipment,
  ornaments, decorations and player items are untouched, the composition keeps open sand for the
  player's pieces, and the colonies are the tank's species at aquarium scale, unlike the outer
  reef's wall and drop-off.

## 10. Known limitations

- Colonies are placed by rules on each formation (sites, spacing, density), not tuned one by one.
- The rock reads a little lumpy at the middle distance level; the formations still follow the
  accepted hulls, so some masses keep a rounded outline.
- The sea fans and soft corals of the plant system keep their library templates.
- The crevice outcrop (the passage) and the glass backdrop reliefs are unchanged.
- `tools/art/pass083/layout.json` (the exported layout, 7.7 MB) is committed so the build is
  reproducible without the game.
