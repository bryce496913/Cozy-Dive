# Pass 107 — MacBook Air player acceptance checklist

**Status: awaiting the user's playthrough and approval. This is not acceptance or a release.**

Use the existing browser profile/save. Record the MacBook Air model/chip, RAM, macOS, browser/version, window/display resolution and power mode. Start at **Full detail**; the existing **Lighter** option changes resolution, not creature models. Discovery hints are in [the spoiler matrix](PASS_107_COLLECTION_MATRIX.md), not in normal gameplay.

- [ ] **Album navigation:** Open Field album. Visit all six habitat tabs, turn pages with buttons and arrow/Page Up/Page Down keys, open/back out of a card, close/reopen the album. The page and selected card should return appropriately; Escape closes the card before the book.
- [ ] **Existing progress:** Check several older discoveries and your existing fish/visitors/layout. Known species must stay unlocked; missing discoveries should be anonymous mystery cards. Counts should match the [acceptance report](PASS_107_ACCEPTANCE_REPORT.md).
- [ ] **Hidden discoveries:** Observe a representative animal in each environment using the matrix hints. Hold it in view close by. Check its card unlocks in the correct primary habitat. Spanish shawl, sea angel and giant isopod reuse their older records; do not expect duplicate slots.
- [ ] **3D cards:** Inspect markings and anatomy, turn the model with drag/buttons, and watch its quiet idle animation. Thin limbs/appendages should remain readable. Check the 22-row matrix as time permits; note any identity or appearance mismatch.
- [ ] **Add and return:** From For your tank → Open the field album, add/return an ordinary fish, a small rare creature and a large visitor. Large miniatures must retain their doubled sizes. Returning an animal must not relock its discovery. If arrival fails, the card should remain unlocked and offer retry/return.
- [ ] **Tank appearance and behavior:** Compare the same species in the wild, on its card and in the tank. Lighting, pose and presentation scale may differ; model/markings should match. Watch bottom animals, sheltered residents, hovering pipefish/seadragons and drifting sea angels. Check boundaries, rocks, neighboring animals and spacing without shrinking large visitors.
- [ ] **Cave shark propulsion:** Approach a resting whitetip and the nurse shark. Watch from above/side as each leaves the sand, accelerates with visible tail beats, slows and resettles. Repeat several times. Look for frozen tails, sideways sliding, clipping or failure to settle.
- [ ] **Reload:** After adding/returning several creatures, reload in the same browser profile. Check old/new discoveries, fish counts, visitor identities and decorations. Exact roaming positions are not saved by the existing format; a changed roaming position alone is not lost progress.
- [ ] **Performance:** Browse several album pages repeatedly and observe the populated tank. Use Settings → Show frame time if useful. Record actual Air behavior, stalls, page/preview loading and any degradation after repeated open/close or add/return. Try Lighter separately if needed; note which setting you used.
- [ ] **Comfort:** Check page turns, camera motion and animal animation at your preferred settings. Enable Reduced camera motion and confirm the calmer presentation is comfortable; manual card rotation should still work.
