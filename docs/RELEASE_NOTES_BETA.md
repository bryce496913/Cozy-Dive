# Cozy Diver — public beta release notes (draft)

_Draft for the owner's approval. The version number and date are filled in at release._

## Welcome

Cozy Diver is a quiet underwater world in your browser. There are no scores, quests, timers or accounts. Start in a small glass office with an aquarium, dive in, and swim at your own pace. A hidden crack leads beyond the tank, to a reef wall over the blue, a kelp forest, a sea cave, the waters under the Antarctic ice, and an old shipwreck at the bottom of a hidden sinkhole.

## What's in this beta

- **Six places to swim:** the home aquarium, the outer reef, the kelp forest, the sea cave, under the ice and the shipwreck, linked by hidden passages, and all of them reachable back home again.
- **Wildlife:**
  - reef fish, turtles, sharks and rays;
  - wall visitors such as manta rays and whale sharks;
  - sea lions, otters and seals in the kelp;
  - resting sharks and lobsters in the cave;
  - penguins, seals (and a pup) and rare visitors under the ice;
  - a wreck overgrown like the SS Yongala: groupers, trevally, barracuda, turtles and clouds of reef fish, with a torch that comes on by itself inside the ship.
- **Seen in the outer waters:** a gentle list of what you've come across. Some things you see can come home to your tank.
- **Your tank:** add fish, corals, plants and stones from the pause screen.
- **Comfort settings:** mouse sensitivity, swimming speed and feel, reduced camera motion, field of view, invert look, graphics detail (Full or Lighter), volume and mute.

## Known limitations

- **Desktop only.** Needs a mouse or trackpad and a keyboard; phones and touch-only tablets show an explanation. A tablet with a mouse or trackpad may work, but is untested.
- **Browsers:** built and tested in Chrome. Safari, Firefox and Edge should work, but haven't been tested yet; please tell us how they go.
- **Graphics:** needs WebGL 2 and hardware acceleration. On modest laptops, try Settings → Graphics detail → Lighter. Entering a new place can cause a brief hitch the first time.
- **Download:** about 16 MB for the first visit (the office and tank), up to about 34 MB once every place has been explored. It's cached afterwards.
- **Saves** live in this browser on this device. Clearing site data, private windows or another browser start afresh. Nothing is stored online.
- **Sound** starts after your first click (browsers require it).

## Privacy

- Cozy Diver keeps your settings, tank and sightings in your browser's local storage only.
- It sends no analytics and loads nothing from other sites.
- Feedback is entirely optional. The "Copy details" button in Settings → Beta feedback copies only the version, browser, graphics, window size and (if you turned it on) the frame-time readout, and only if you choose to paste them.

## Beta feedback and testing plan

**Who:** a small group of invited testers first (about 10–20), then a wider beta once no blocker has appeared for two weeks.

**What we'd love to hear about:**

1. **First five minutes:** was it clear what to do? Did anything feel confusing or rushed?
2. **Calm:** did anything feel stressful, too dark, too bright, too loud or too sudden?
3. **Getting around:** did you find the hidden passages? Did you ever feel stuck or lost?
4. **Smoothness:** your device, and whether swimming felt smooth (Settings → Show frame time shows the numbers).
5. **Anything broken:** what happened, where you were, and what you expected.

**How:** Settings → Beta feedback → Copy details (optional), then Open the feedback form.

**Rhythm:**

- Week 1: invited testers, with the playthrough checklist.
- Weeks 2–3: open feedback.
- Then a summary, fixes, and a decision on the next beta.

Blockers (see [BETA_READINESS.md](BETA_READINESS.md#release-blocker-checklist)) lead to a rollback and a fix; everything else is gathered for the next release.
